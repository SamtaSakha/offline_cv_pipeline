# License and Provenance

## Software Components
- **OpenCV (cv2):** Used for image decoding, Haar Cascade detection, and basic image operations. Licensed under Apache 2.0.
- **scikit-image:** Used for Local Binary Pattern (LBP) feature extraction. Licensed under Modified BSD.
- **NumPy:** Used for array manipulation and math. Licensed under BSD.

## Training Data and Models
- **Haar Cascade (`haarcascade_frontalface_default.xml`):** 
  - Bundled natively with OpenCV.
  - License: Intel/OpenCV standard open-source license.
- **LBP Embedder:**
  - Zero-weight algorithm. It is a fixed mathematical transform published in 2006 (Ahonen et al.).
  - No training data was used, eliminating any GDPR or dataset-scraping licensing risks.
